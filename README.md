# The World Today — 20 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026, for the
make.fun bounty **news-image-20261120** ("Sum up 20 November's world news in
one image").

## The stories in the picture

- **Trump: no Iran decision until 2027** — The US president says he will
  "see what happens" after January 1, pushing any decision on war with
  Iran into next year.
- **Cyclone Akvan hits India** — The severe storm makes landfall on the
  Odisha coast by noon, with 300,000 people evacuated to shelters.
- **GTA VI passes $1.8 billion** — The game sells about 19 million copies
  in its first day, overtaking the biggest film opening ever.
- **Europe ready for Russia talks** — Leaders at the Berlin summit agree a
  united negotiating position on ending the war in Ukraine.
- **Also today:** South Korea's top court overturns ex-PM Han's 15-year
  term; Fiji declares a drought disaster; US intelligence says Iran still
  holds 2,500 missiles.

## How it runs

The poster is a single 1024x1024 PNG held in this repository. The program
copies it to the output directory as `entry.png`:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
